Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our healthcare services

How do I schedule an appointment?

You can schedule an appointment by clicking the "Schedule Appointment" button on any provider's profile page. You can also call the provider's office directly using the phone number listed on their profile.

What insurance plans do you accept?

Our providers accept a variety of insurance plans including Blue Cross Blue Shield, Aetna, UnitedHealthcare, Cigna, Humana, Medicare, and Medicaid. Please check each provider's profile to see which specific insurance plans they accept.

Do you offer telehealth services?

Yes! Many of our providers offer telehealth appointments. Look for the "Telehealth Available" badge on provider profiles. Telehealth appointments allow you to consult with your provider from the comfort of your home.

What should I bring to my first appointment?

Please bring a valid photo ID, your insurance card, a list of current medications, and any relevant medical records from previous providers. If you're a new patient, you may also need to arrive 15 minutes early to complete paperwork.

How can I access my medical records?

You can request your medical records by contacting your provider's office directly. Many providers also offer patient portals where you can access your records online 24/7.

What is your cancellation policy?

We ask that you provide at least 24 hours notice if you need to cancel or reschedule an appointment. This allows us to offer the time slot to other patients who may need care.

Do you see walk-in patients?

Walk-in availability varies by provider and location. We recommend calling ahead to check availability. For urgent care needs, we can often accommodate same-day appointments.

How do I get a prescription refill?

Contact your provider's office directly to request a prescription refill. Many providers also offer prescription refill requests through their patient portal.
