Accessibility Features

We're committed to making healthcare accessible for everyone

Our Accessibility Commitment

Carrollton Healthcare is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We are continually improving the user experience for everyone and applying the relevant accessibility standards to achieve these goals.

This website conforms to WCAG 2.1 Level AA standards to provide an accessible experience for all users.

Accessibility Settings

Click the gear icon (⚙️) in the bottom right corner to access our accessibility settings:

  • Color Themes: Choose from Default, Dark Mode, Warm Tones, or High Contrast to suit your visual preferences
  • Font Size: Adjust text size to Normal, Large, or Extra Large for better readability
  • Persistent Settings: Your preferences are saved in your browser for future visits

Keyboard Navigation

Our website is fully navigable using only a keyboard:

  • Tab: Move forward through interactive elements
  • Shift + Tab: Move backward through interactive elements
  • Enter: Activate links and buttons
  • Skip to Content: Press Tab when the page loads to reveal a "Skip to main content" link

Screen Reader Support

Our website is optimized for screen readers with proper ARIA labels, semantic HTML, and descriptive text. We've tested compatibility with popular screen readers including JAWS, NVDA, and VoiceOver.

Additional Features

  • Clear focus indicators for keyboard navigation
  • High contrast text and backgrounds for readability
  • Descriptive link text and button labels
  • Alternative text for all images
  • Consistent navigation across all pages
  • Mobile-friendly responsive design

Physical Accessibility

Our healthcare facilities are designed to be accessible to all patients:

  • Wheelchair accessible entrances and exam rooms
  • Accessible parking spaces
  • Elevators in multi-story buildings
  • Braille and tactile signage
  • Sign language interpreters available upon request
  • Assistive listening devices

Feedback

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of this website. If you encounter any accessibility barriers or have suggestions for improvement, please contact us at accessibility@carrolltonhealthcare.com or call (678) 790-8163.
