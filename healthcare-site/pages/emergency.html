Urgent and emergency care

Emergency services

Know when to call 911, when urgent care is a better fit, and where to find emergency care near Carrollton. This page is for education only—if you are unsure, call 911 or seek care in person.

In life-threatening emergencies, call 911 immediately

Do not use the contact form, email, or this website for time-sensitive or emergency help. If you or someone else may be in danger, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room.

When to call 911

Use emergency services (911) for conditions that can cause lasting harm or are immediately life-threatening.

Common 911 reasons

  • Chest pain, pressure, or trouble breathing
  • Signs of stroke (sudden weakness, confusion, slurred speech, face drooping)
  • Severe bleeding that does not stop with pressure
  • Serious injury, bad burns, or loss of consciousness

Urgent care guidance (non–life-threatening)

Urgent care can handle many same-day needs when you are not in an emergency. Examples include:

  • Minor sprains, small cuts, and simple fractures
  • Fever, earaches, and flu symptoms when you cannot reach your regular doctor
  • After-hours help for non-severe issues
  • Non-severe rashes, infections, and minor animal bites (when not severe)

If you are not sure, call 911 or the closest ER—when in doubt, err on the side of emergency care.
